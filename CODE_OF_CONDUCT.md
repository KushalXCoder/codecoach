# Code of Conduct

All participants in this project are expected to uphold a welcoming, inclusive, and respectful community. The following is a brief code of conduct; maintainers may elect to adopt the Contributor Covenant in full.

**Expected behavior**
- Be respectful and considerate in language and actions.
- Provide constructive feedback and be open to discussion.
- Give credit where it's due and acknowledge contributors.

**Unacceptable behavior**
- Harassment, hateful language, discriminatory remarks, or threats.
- Publicly shaming or demeaning other contributors.

**Reporting**
- If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, please contact the project maintainers at the repository contact or open an issue with the label `security` or `moderation`.

Maintainers will review reports confidentially and take appropriate action, which may include warnings, removal of contributions, or blocking users from the repository.

Thank you for helping keep this project welcoming and productive.
